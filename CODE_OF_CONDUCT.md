# Code of Conduct

We want Updraft to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, assume good intent,
and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private
information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributions that don't follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful.

**Reporting:** use GitHub's "Report content" feature or open a private security advisory
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and mark it as a conduct report.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
